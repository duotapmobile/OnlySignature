Only Signature creates signature image assets. It does not upload or edit documents, require an account, offer a subscription, or use a backend.

Quick review path:
1. Tap Get Started.
2. Draw a signature, initials, or both. Either slot alone is allowed.
3. Tap Continue and compare the same fictional agreement with white and transparent backgrounds.
4. Tap Confirm.
5. On Transparent Export, choose the visible free white-background export or purchase transparent export.

One consumable purchase covers the current local signature-plus-initials set and one later unused companion slot. Re-exporting the same saved set does not repurchase. Purchased artwork is local and immutable; changing strokes creates a new draft. There is no Restore Purchase control because a consumed purchase cannot reconstruct locally deleted artwork. Unfinished transactions are observed at launch and finished only after durable local binding.

The app supports portrait and landscape drawing while preserving both slots. Privacy, Terms, support, purchase FAQ, data/storage behavior, and Delete All Saved Signatures are in Settings. Delete All is unavailable while a purchase is pending or recovering.

Mock StoreKit exists only in development/fixture builds. Production configuration fails if mock mode or screenshot fixtures are enabled.
