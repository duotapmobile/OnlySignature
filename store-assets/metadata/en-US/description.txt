Create your handwritten signature and initials, then export clean image files for the documents and apps you already use.

Only Signature is an asset maker, not a document-signing platform. It never asks you to upload a contract, PDF, form, invoice, or other private document.

SEE THE DIFFERENCE
Preview the same signature on the same fictional document with a white background and with a transparent background. The transparent version keeps nearby words and lines visible.

EXPORT WITHOUT THE WHITE BOX
Choose PNG with a transparent background when you need a signature that sits naturally on a page. Choose a white-background PNG or JPEG when that is what your destination needs. JPEG always uses a white background because JPEG does not support transparency.

SIGNATURE + INITIALS
One transparent-set purchase includes one signature slot and one initials slot. Add both before purchase or fill the unused included slot later without paying again.

NO SUBSCRIPTION
The planned U.S. price is $1.99 for transparent export of one signature-and-initials set. Apple displays the actual localized price before purchase. Re-export that saved set in any supported format without another charge. A changed drawing is a new set.

A USEFUL FREE OPTION
Save full-quality white-background PNG or JPEG files for free. The free export is not intentionally blurred or reduced in quality.

CREATED ON YOUR DEVICE
Only Signature creates and stores reusable drawings locally. We do not automatically upload signature content, and the app has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, or developer cloud storage. A destination you choose through Apple’s sharing tools may store or send your exported file.

IMPORTANT LOCAL-STORAGE LIMIT
Deleting the app may delete sets stored inside it. Files you already exported remain wherever you saved them. A consumed purchase cannot recreate deleted signature artwork.

AUTHORIZED USE ONLY
Create or store only signatures you are authorized to use. Only Signature does not verify identity, notarize documents, issue certificates, create cryptographic digital signatures, provide an audit trail, give legal advice, guarantee legal enforceability, or guarantee that a recipient will accept an image signature.
