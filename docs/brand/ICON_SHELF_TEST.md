# Icon Shelf Test

## Purpose

Select a production-safe opaque icon that recognizes signature transparency without resembling a bank, legal-signing suite, or competitor. This document is the test authority; a pass must reference actual generated files and hashes.

## Concepts

- **Integrated-text:** dark teal, frosted circular lens/check motif, handwritten stroke/line, short `TRANSPARENT` or `TRANSPARENT EXPORT` treatment.
- **No-text fallback:** same distinctive lens/check/stroke construction without small lettering.

## Shelf set

Compare beside the most relevant live signature-asset and sign-document apps recorded in the competitor matrix, including subscription-heavy signature makers, transparent-PNG utilities, and major e-signing results. Do not copy their layout, orange/blue dominance, nib/pen symbols, or signature-letter monograms.

## Render matrix

- 1024×1024 source and App Store view
- App Store search-result approximation
- iPhone Home Screen
- Spotlight
- Settings
- grayscale and blurred peripheral view
- light/dark wallpaper contexts
- high/low brightness and increased contrast

## Acceptance

- Opaque RGB output with no alpha.
- Recognizable as signature/transparency asset, not bank/security scanner.
- Primary stroke/lens remains distinct at Settings/Search size.
- Text, if used, is readable immediately without zoom; otherwise select no-text.
- No competitor trade-dress confusion in a five-second shelf review.
- Clean silhouette and restrained internal detail; no visual noise from checker motif.
- Required asset-catalog sizes generate without clipping, transparency, color-profile, or interpolation defects.
