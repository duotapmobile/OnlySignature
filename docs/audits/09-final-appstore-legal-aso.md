## Verdict

**NO-GO FOR TESTFLIGHT, APP STORE CONNECT SUBMISSION, PUBLIC LAUNCH, OR A “RELEASE READY” CLAIM. GO FOR LOCAL REMEDIATION.**

No P0 was found. Eleven P1 groups remain. Nine include locally fixable implementation or release-package work; five also include genuine founder, legal, Apple-environment, or portal gates, with overlap between those sets. The repository has a viable narrow product and a strong preparation package, but the actual implementation does not yet satisfy the prior final authority’s purchase-safety, pasteboard, optional-format, pre-purchase disclosure, content-authority, and production-configuration decisions.

The strongest positive evidence is real: the static site builds and validates with zero client JavaScript or tracking patterns; the legal drafts are fact-specific and appropriately caveated; current metadata lengths pass Apple’s limits; the 20-app competitor matrix and 30-row keyword map meet the research scope; the selected EAS image is currently available and satisfies Apple’s Xcode/SDK floor; the final app icon is an opaque 1024×1024 RGB PNG and matches the mobile asset byte-for-byte; and the repository correctly continues to describe release as NO-GO in `docs/RELEASE_BLOCKERS.md:1-3`.

## Legal and privacy drift assessment

The substantive legal boundary is otherwise sound. `legal/TERMS_OF_USE.md:11-42` consistently says the product creates an image asset, prohibits unauthorized use, and disclaims identity verification, certification, notarization, audit trail, enforceability, and universal acceptance. The Privacy Policy distinguishes automatic app behavior, user-selected exports, Apple commerce, support messages, and website logs. The website renders the canonical legal Markdown rather than a separately rewritten policy. `npm run check:content` confirmed the generated legal copy hashes are synchronized.

That good drafting does not cure implementation drift. The pasteboard statement must be corrected with C-FINAL-03, the paywall must receive the pre-purchase deletion disclosure in C-FINAL-05, and publication remains blocked until final operator facts, actual support/hosting practices, selected regions, signed-binary behavior, and counsel review are known. No policy is attorney-certified, and this audit is not legal advice.

## Website assessment

The static Astro site is materially complete: Home, Privacy, Terms, Support, FAQ, Accessibility, Contact, download placeholder, 404, sitemap, robots, responsive navigation, metadata, Open Graph, structured data, favicon, and mailto support are present. The actual validation run built nine HTML pages plus robots/sitemap, resolved internal links, found zero client JavaScript, and found no forbidden tracking/font/cookie patterns. This supports the no-backend/no-tracking website claim.

`npm --prefix apps/site run release:check` correctly failed on placeholder domain, operator, address, support identity, public URL, and governing law. That is expected safe behavior, not a website implementation failure. Do not deploy the placeholder build or put its `.invalid` URLs into App Store Connect.

## ASO, competitor, and name assessment

The research package is adequate for a version 1.0 draft: `research/aso/COMPETITOR_MATRIX.csv` contains 20 directly relevant U.S. listings with 23 fields; `research/aso/KEYWORD_MAP.csv` contains 30 candidates with 11 fields; review mining distinguishes observed reviews from inference; PPO hypotheses are genuinely different; localization remains staged rather than machine-publishing legal text. Ratings are treated as demand proxies, not downloads, and no download, revenue, keyword-volume, conversion, retention, or market-share figures are invented.

The product-page position is defensible: real handwriting assets, transparent PNG, no document upload, no login, no subscription, no editing/cropping, and a per-set purchase. It is differentiated from document-signing suites and subscription-heavy makers. Commercial viability remains an experiment, not a proven conclusion. App Store Connect product-page metrics, proceeds, ratings/reviews, and later-authorized Apple Search Ads data are the correct privacy-safe measurement sources.

“Only Signature” remains a working name only. The preliminary screen is useful but is expressly not a legal opinion. The registered natural domain and incomplete comprehensive clearance are release gates, not reasons to silently rename the repository.
