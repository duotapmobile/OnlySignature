# Regional Release Checklist

**Status:** Legal preparation, not legal advice. Distribution remains disabled outside an explicit founder territory selection.

## Common prerequisites

- Final operator identity/address/contact and business status
- Fact-specific Privacy Policy and Terms reviewed for selected territories
- App/website/support processors, host logs, retention, rights request path, and international transfers documented
- Clear per-set price, no-subscription, local deletion, Apple refund, and authorized-use disclosures
- Export-compliance, tax/banking, age rating, App Privacy, accessibility, and store seller information complete
- No claim of certified/legally binding/notarized/universally accepted signature

## United States

- E-SIGN claim boundary: app creates an image asset and does not execute/attach/authenticate a document.
- Consumer-protection review of paywall, free path, deletion, new-set charge, and refunds.
- Apple Paid Applications, tax/banking, U.S. price, support/privacy URLs.
- COPPA: general utility, not child-directed; no unnecessary age collection; reassess actual knowledge/data flows.

## California

- CalOPPA conspicuous accurate policy assessment.
- CCPA/CPRA applicability based on actual operator thresholds/practices; do not claim statutory applicability or build unnecessary “sell/share” machinery without determination.
- Maintain factual no sale/share-for-targeted-advertising statement only while practices support it.

## European Union

- GDPR/controller identity, lawful processing of website/support data, rights/contact, retention, processors/transfers tailored to actual facts.
- DSA trader/non-trader declaration. If trader, verify public address/phone/email displayed by Apple.
- Consumer price/refund/precontract disclosures and territory/language review.
- eIDAS boundary: output is not advanced/qualified signature, certificate, trust service, or legal-effect guarantee.
- EU territory remains disabled until founder/legal completion.

## United Kingdom

- UK GDPR/Data Protection Act applicability, controller/contact, rights, processors/transfers, retention, and ICO obligations assessed.
- Consumer purchase/refund and electronic-signature claim review.
- UK distribution explicitly selected; EU DSA completion does not substitute for UK review.

## Canada

- PIPEDA/provincial privacy applicability and meaningful-consent/contact/retention procedures assessed.
- Consumer price/refund and language/Quebec requirements assessed for selected provinces.
- Signature-image acceptance remains recipient/law dependent.

## Australia

- Privacy Act/APP applicability and operator threshold/exceptions assessed.
- Australian Consumer Law price, misleading-representation, refund, warranty/limitation language reviewed.
- Electronic-transactions requirements and recipient acceptance not guaranteed.

## Evidence and stop rule

Record counsel/date/territories, final policies, portal availability, DSA evidence, public contact display, processor/host facts, and consumer disclosures. If required operator facts or legal determination is absent, exclude that territory rather than publish generic worldwide-compliance claims.
