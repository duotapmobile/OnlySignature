# Preliminary Name Clearance — Only Signature

Research date: 2026-08-25  
Working mark: **Only Signature**  
Scope: United States launch screening, with limited global web/domain observations  
Status: **material unresolved risk; keep the working name centralized but do not treat it as legally cleared**

> This is a preliminary product-team screening, not a legal opinion, trademark clearance, registration guarantee, or freedom-to-operate opinion. A U.S.-licensed trademark attorney should complete and document the final search before public launch or material brand spend.

## Executive finding

No exact `Only Signature` iOS app-name match surfaced in the live U.S. App Store searches performed on 2026-08-25. That helps App Store distinctiveness only weakly: the shelf contains many confusingly similar descriptive names built from “Signature,” “Maker,” “Creator,” “Scanner,” and “eSign.” More importantly, `onlysignature.com` is registered and has documented prior commercial use by Signature Real Estate Companies. The exact federal-trademark result was not conclusively cleared in this run, and public social-handle indexing was insufficient to prove availability.

Disposition: continue building as **Only Signature** because brand strings are centralized, but make final attorney clearance, founder name approval, domain choice, and handle reservation release blockers.

Confidence: medium for the sampled storefront on the research date. App availability and names can change before submission.

### Federal trademark records

The official [USPTO Trademark Search](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search) and current search-system guidance were checked. Exact-word indexed searches did not surface a reliable federal record for `ONLY SIGNATURE`, but the interactive federal database did not yield an exportable, reproducible result set in this run. Therefore this report does **not** state that no exact or similar federal mark exists.

USPTO warns that an adequate search must look beyond identical wording. Marks can conflict through similarity in sound, appearance, meaning, or commercial impression when the goods/services are related. A comprehensive search also includes pending/registered federal marks, state registries, common-law use, domains, WIPO/EUIPO/TMview, and the internet. See [Comprehensive clearance search](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks), [Federal trademark searching](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching), and [Likelihood of confusion](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion).

Required counsel search should include:

- exact and normalized forms: `ONLY SIGNATURE`, `ONLYSIGNATURE`, `ONLY SIGN`, and plural/possessive variants;
- phonetic, spacing, and commercial-impression variants;
- coordinated classes and related goods, not only one class;
- at minimum Class 009 downloadable mobile software and related image/signature/document utilities; counsel should determine any additional related classes from the final goods description;
- live federal records plus relevant dead marks still used in commerce;
- the final icon/wordmark through USPTO design-code/image searching.

Confidence: high that full clearance remains required; low on any negative federal conclusion.

### Common-law and commercial use

An indexed 2021 page published by **Signature Real Estate Companies** links to `http://onlysignature.com` as a company destination. Evidence: [Vimeo page, lines identifying the company and domain](https://vimeo.com/510337303). This is evidence of prior public use of the exact domain/string in real-estate services; it is not evidence of software trademark ownership or legal conflict by itself.

The goods/services appear different from a downloadable signature-image utility, which reduces but does not eliminate practical or legal risk. The domain collision creates unavoidable traffic, support, credibility, and brand-control friction even if counsel finds no likelihood of confusion.

### Famous-brand and trade-dress considerations

- The word `Only` can prompt an association with famous “Only…” brands, most obviously OnlyFans, even though the products and full marks differ. No evidence found here establishes legal confusion.
- Do not use OnlyFans terms, typography, blue palette, iconography, adult themes, search keywords, or comparison copy. The controlling product contract already prohibits exploiting that search traffic.
- The locked dark-teal frosted-lens/checker-transparency direction should also be shelf-tested against signature competitors. Avoid SignNow-like blue, Signature Maker orange, a generic white cursive “S,” and pen/nib motifs that could create category sameness or copied trade dress.
- Search the final icon through USPTO image search and general reverse-image search before release.

## Three fallback candidates

These are centralized-string alternatives only. None is cleared or adopted.

1. **ClearStroke Signature** — communicates clean output and real strokes without claiming certification. Exact web/App Store screening did not surface a direct signature-app brand on 2026-08-25. Counsel must still search `ClearStroke`, `Clear Stroke`, phonetic variants, and related software.
2. **TrueAlpha Signature** — communicates actual transparency to design-literate users while retaining category recognition. A web result for “TrueAlpha” in an unrelated technical identity context surfaced, so this candidate needs especially careful related-goods and common-law review.
3. **StrokeOnly** — preserves the narrow-product idea while being more distinctive than a generic “signature maker.” Exact screening surfaced generic code terminology rather than a direct app brand, but domain, mark, and handle clearance remain open.

Rejected during preliminary ideation: `AlphaScribe` (existing document/AI uses), `InkLayer` (existing software/art uses), and `ClearMark Signature` (existing ClearMark/e-signature usage) carry more obvious collision risk.

## Release gate

Do not file, submit, purchase broad marketing inventory, or publish the final icon/website until all of the following are recorded:

1. attorney-reviewed federal, state, common-law, domain, international, and design clearance;
2. final owner/legal entity and accurate goods/services description;
3. same-day App Store name recheck;
4. authorized domain acquisition and DNS control;
5. logged-in social handle checks/reservations;
6. final icon shelf/reverse-image/trade-dress review;
7. founder’s explicit final-name decision.

Until then, keep `Only Signature` as the working name and retain centralized brand strings so a defensible rename does not require product logic changes.
